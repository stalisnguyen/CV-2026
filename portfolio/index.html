Why I build

Retention is a product decision. So I build products.

Self-learners rarely fail for lack of content. They fail because motivation fades, there's no clear path, feedback is slow, and they have to stitch together a flashcard app, a question bank and a video course themselves. I wanted one place that tells you what to do today, explains every answer, and makes it easy to keep going with a small study group, at close to zero running cost.

So I built four self-study apps on one shared engine and an AI reflection app, owning the product definition, the content and the build. Each has its own learner, its own way to measure progress and its own retention loop. It's lifecycle thinking applied to product, and it's the same approach I bring to teams I work with.

How I build

One lightweight engine shared by all four learning apps: a framework-free single-page app, installable as a PWA, with serverless sync and AI endpoints, running on free tiers.

  1. Define the learnerWho they are, their level and language, and how progress is measured.
  2. Spec the contentA content spec with validators, then parallel AI agents draft content that must pass validation before handover.
  3. Build on the shared engineProduct-specific logic, like question types, labs and scoring models, lives in its own module.
  4. Test headlessEvery screen tested at phone and desktop widths, key flows tested end to end, and maths and code answers re-checked in Python.
  5. Ship & iterateDeploy, read the logs and fix what real use reveals, such as mobile speaking, inflated placement bands and sync conflicts.

Echolis · Inner Voices

LIVE · WEB + APP STORE

"You are not one voice. You are a conversation." An IFS-inspired reflection space: you write about a real moment, your parts answer in their own voices (the Protector, the Wounded Child, the Analyst and the Hidden Part), and an Observer leaves you one question.

💬 Inner conflict, not a cheerleader

Most AI companions offer one agreeable bot. Echolis splits what you write across your inner parts, which reply to each other, so you see the conflict instead of just being soothed.

❓ Asks, doesn't advise

The Observer ends each reflection with one question, not a fix or a verdict, so the insight work stays yours instead of depending on the app.

🚪 Three doors in

Free or guided Reflection, a step-by-step Emotional Debugger for reactions that felt too big, and a Decision Council for choices you keep circling.

🧠 Memory you control

Parts remember earlier sessions and patterns surface over time. You can see and delete your memories.

🌏 Trilingual from day one

Built in English, Vietnamese and 中文 from the start rather than translated later, so the parts sound natural in each language.

📊 Designed for retention

Built with a lifecycle mindset, with a weekly product scorecard (retention, active writers) and lifecycle messaging designed in.

🛡️ Safety-first product decisions
  • 16+ age floor, the same across the app, the Terms and the App Store rating, with extra safeguards for 16–17-year-olds.
  • Crisis detection tested on single messages and on crises that build gradually. It points people to their local emergency or hotline numbers, while ordinary venting doesn't set it off.
  • Shame and fear replies reassure first, use the person's own words, offer at most one small next step, and never diagnose.

A reflection space, not therapy. It can sit alongside therapy, and users can choose to share a summary with a practitioner.

Stalong 思龙

Mandarin Chinese, HSK 1 to 7–9 · for Vietnamese speakers
LIVE

A Mandarin course for Vietnamese speakers that runs from HSK 1 to HSK 7–9 on the HSK 3.0 standard. It uses Sino-Vietnamese (Hán Việt) readings as a memory bridge, and Tiểu Long, a baby dragon mascot, guides the way.

  • Smart flashcards: spaced repetition with tone-coloured pinyin, Hán Việt readings, Vietnamese meanings and example sentences, plus a dictionary you can search by hanzi, toneless pinyin, Hán Việt or Vietnamese.
  • Full curriculum: stroke-order writing practice, the 214 radicals, grammar notes flagging mistakes Vietnamese learners often make, and HSK mock tests for each level with scoring and explanations.
  • Speak it out loud: scenario-based AI conversations with corrections in Vietnamese, and shadowing practice that records you, transcribes your speech and compares it with the model sentence.
  • Habit loop: imperial-exam titles from Đồng sinh to Trạng nguyên, streaks, review games, a mistake log and a weekly group leaderboard.

Catalis

IELTS Academic & General Training · band 4.5 to 9
LIVE

IELTS prep for Vietnamese learners, from band 4.5 to 9, coached by Jack, an orange cat the app also calls Mít. It is built around honest progress, so it never gives you a flattering band score.

  • Objective placement: an adaptive vocabulary test with grammar, timed reading and optional listening, weighted and rounded down. Band estimates are split by skill, and the app tells you plainly when it doesn't have enough data yet.
  • Plan that fits your day: a daily route built from your target band and minutes per day. Listening uses a distinct natural voice for each speaker plus dictation, and the full 40-question Reading mocks convert to a band.
  • AI feedback: Writing and Speaking scored against the four IELTS criteria, with Speaking recorded on phone or desktop. An AI room lets you debate, practise paraphrasing and turn real English text into flashcards.
  • Habit loop: titles from Freshman to Professor, streaks, daily tips, pronunciation drills, games and a mistake log.

Calculis

GMAT Focus Edition · from the basics to 705+
LIVE

GMAT Focus Edition prep covering Quant, Verbal and Data Insights, for complete beginners and 705+ targets alike. Max the fox is your study buddy and AI tutor.

  • Exam-real practice: all 8 question types, including sortable Table Analysis, SVG-drawn charts, multi-tab Multi-Source Reasoning and an on-screen calculator for Data Insights. There are 5 difficulty levels, timed modes, and a worked solution with trap notes for every question.
  • Adaptive mocks: a full 64-question exam with a choice of section order, a 10-minute break and Review & Edit for up to 3 answers. There is also a 15-question diagnostic.
  • Transparent scoring: section (60–90) and total (205–805) estimates from a 3-parameter IRT model, clearly labelled as an estimate rather than GMAC's algorithm.
  • Learning loop: an 81-step "Start here" path and an error log that tags why you missed each question. Max gives up to 3 step-by-step hints without revealing the answer. Titles run from Intern to CEO.

Neuralis

AI & Robotics from zero · career paths
LIVE

Learn AI and robotics from zero, deep enough to understand the field and build a career in it: AI engineering, forward-deployed engineering, robotics or AI product. Lessons are explained in Vietnamese with English terminology, and Bit the robot keeps you company.

  • Six-track skill tree: Python, Math for AI, Machine Learning, LLM & GenAI, Robotics and Career. Each lesson comes with key terms, a 4-question quiz, an auto-graded Python exercise and a linked lab.
  • Python in the browser: code runs in a Web Worker with numpy, auto-stops after 12 seconds to prevent runaway loops, and explains errors in Vietnamese. There is a free-form playground too.
  • 17 interactive labs: tokenizers, temperature and top-p, embeddings, attention, a mini RAG, gradient descent, backprop, robot-arm kinematics, drone PID, A*, Q-learning, Kalman filters and lidar.
  • Neuralis Arcade: the first version was too reading-heavy, so learning games, achievement badges and weekly quests were added as a retention lever: a reason to come back, not just another page to finish.
  • Career map: 14 AI and robotics career paths, each with a day in the life, skills, tools, a starter path, portfolio ideas and interview questions. Bit, the AI assistant, can review your code and plan a route to the path you choose.
