Echolis · Inner Voices

LIVE ON WEB + iOS

"You are not one voice. You are a conversation." Echolis is a quiet AI reflection space inspired by parts work (IFS). You write what's really going on, and the parts of you that are present answer, each in its own voice: the Protector, the Wounded Child, the Analyst and the Hidden Part. An Observer holds the whole picture.

💬 A chat you can talk back to

Parts reply in a group-chat thread, and you can talk to any one of them directly.

🚪 Three doors in

Free or guided Reflection, a step-by-step Emotional Debugger for reactions that felt too big, and a Decision Council for choices you keep circling.

🧠 Memory that deepens

Parts remember earlier sessions, and you can see and delete exactly what Echolis remembers about you.

🗺️ Patterns over time

Triggers and protective cycles surface over weeks as an inner map, alongside a dated history of your sessions.

🤝 Consent-first sharing

You can share a brief with a therapist: summaries only, never your raw writing, and revocable in one tap.

🛡️ Private & safe by design

Private by default, with a no-account guest mode, export and real deletion. Crisis messages surface hotlines for the user's country.

Stalong 思龙

Mandarin Chinese for Vietnamese speakers
LIVE

A Mandarin study app for Vietnamese learners (HSK 1–9), built for a group learning together and guided by Tiểu Long, a little dragon mascot. It runs on a 30-minute daily plan.

  • Daily session: spaced-repetition flashcards, listening and dictation, speaking with tone scoring, a graded reading and a culture story
  • Progression: XP levels named after imperial Chinese exam ranks, plus a word of the day
  • Onboarding: a 10-minute placement test, and progress synced across devices for the group

Catalis

IELTS prep for Vietnamese learners
LIVE

An IELTS Academic and General Training prep app for Vietnamese learners studying as a group, coached by Jack, an orange cat mascot. Every day is one short, balanced session.

  • Daily session: vocab flashcards, one Listening part, one Reading passage, three Speaking answers and a daily tip
  • AI grading: Writing and Speaking scored by AI, rolled into an estimated band against your target
  • Motivation: a placement test, XP levels from Freshman up, and a shared study-group board

Calculis

GMAT Focus Edition prep
LIVE

A GMAT Focus Edition prep app for a study group, with Max the fox as guide. It covers Quant, Verbal and Data Insights in a 45-minute daily plan, from the basics up.

  • Guided path: an 81-step beginner path, topic sets, flashcards, lessons and a 2-minute mental-math sprint
  • Exam realism: adaptive mock exams, a section score estimate and a 15-question diagnostic
  • Learning loop: an error log for tagging mistakes, an AI tutor, and career-style XP levels from Intern to Analyst
